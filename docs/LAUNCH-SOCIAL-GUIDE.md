### Post 2 — The loop

**Format:** Three-post thread

**1/3**

> A mining game used to have one prize: more of its own token.
>
> Stock Miner changes the prize. Rigs mine fragments of tokenized stocks—NVDAx, TSLAx, AAPLx, SPYx—one block at a time.

**2/3**

> Stake $RIG to activate a rig. Burn $RIG for GPUs, cooling, and overclocks to raise its hashrate.
>
> Every second a rig runs, its hashrate becomes work. Every unit of work pays a fixed number of fragments. Nobody's rate moves when someone else joins.

**3/3**

> A block is found when the mine has done enough work. Not when a timer runs out.
>
> Four blocks. Then the mine closes forever. Stakes come back. Fragments redeem for whole Stock Tokens or USDC.
>
> One season. One mine. One final block.

## Copy guardrails

These follow `docs/07-COMPLIANCE-AND-RISK.md`. Apply them to every post and every reply.

- Never promise yield or returns. Rewards depend on other players' hashrate and the pool. Say "share of the pool", never "APY" or "earn X%".
- Every duration is an estimate. Write "about", "sized for", or "est." next to any time figure. Never post a countdown.
- Say the burn out loud. Any post that mentions upgrades says the $RIG is burned and non-refundable.
- Say the close out loud. Any post that mentions rewards says the mine closes when block 4 is found or at the cap.
- Fragments are non-transferable in v1. Do not describe them as tradeable, and do not call them tokens of the underlying stock.
- Redemption for Stock Tokens is for eligible wallets only. Everyone else cashes out in USDC. Do not imply everyone receives shares.
- The numbers in Day 2 Post 1 are the worked example from the game design doc, not a live season. Label them as an example if asked.
- No price talk. Do not comment on the $RIG price, Stock Token prices, or the USD value of a claim.
