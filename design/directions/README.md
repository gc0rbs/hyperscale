# Directions – "it needs a bit more something"

The first two concept sets were clean but generic: a colour system and a tick bar, no signature.
Three candidates for the missing thing, each shown on the same surfaces (poster, share card, landing
hero, splash) so they compare like for like. Generated with Higgsfield, palette-constrained.

| Direction | Files | The idea | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The seam** | `31`–`34` | A jagged amber vein runs through surfaces; where it opens, cyan shows through. | Ownable; literally the product (a vein of ore, fragments); flat and vector, so it is cheap to build in SVG/CSS and can be *animated*: the seam can grow across the block card as work accumulates and split open cyan when the block is found. Works as a brand device and as UI. | Needs discipline: one seam per surface, or it becomes decoration. |
| **B. Material** | `35`–`38` | Brushed metal, etched type, inlaid enamel stripes, raking light. | Premium, tactile, photographs beautifully for marketing and merch. | Photographic; does not translate to a live UI without heavy imagery, and fights the light shell. Best kept for physical goods and hero shots. |
| **C. Fragments** | `39`–`42` | Faceted amber gem, shards in motion, cyan edge light. | Explains "fragments" instantly; strong for the block-found moment. | Reads as generic 3D crypto; the gem competes with the type for attention. |

## Recommendation

**A, the seam, as the brand's signature device**, with B reserved for physical goods and C reduced to
the single block-found gem. Concretely:

- The seam becomes the progress indicator on the block card: it advances across the card as work
  accumulates (the tick marks are its branches) and splits open cyan at 100%. That makes the brand
  device and the core game readout the same thing.
- Marketing surfaces carry one seam each, never more.
- The tick bar stays as the compact form of the seam where a jagged line would be too loud (tables,
  notifications, small components).
- The gem from C appears once, at block found, as the reward object.

If accepted, the brief gets a "Signature device" section and the tokens gain a seam SVG path and a
motion spec; the block card mockup is redone with the seam.
