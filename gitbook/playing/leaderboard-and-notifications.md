# Leaderboard, notifications and sharing

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks wallets by total base hashrate, by fragments claimed, and by $RIG burned. Overclock boosts are excluded from the hash ranking because they are transient. The rankings come from an indexer and can lag the chain by a few seconds; your own numbers on the mine page are read from the chain directly.

## Notifications

Blocks are found on no schedule, so the mine page offers **Notify me**. It uses your browser's notification permission and fires when:

* a block is found (with the stock and a reminder to claim),
* the mine closes,
* a new shift starts, in long-haul mode only.

Notifications are local to the browser tab: they work while a Hyperscale tab is open, in the background is fine. They do not need an account and nothing is stored outside your browser. On iOS Safari, notifications require the site to be added to the home screen.

## Sharing

When a block is found, the banner has a **Share** button. On mobile it opens the system share sheet; elsewhere it copies a line like "Block 2 found in Hyperscale: I mined 0.412 MU on Robinhood Chain" with the site link. Links to the site render a share card that shows the live state of the current season.
