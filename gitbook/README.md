# Welcome to Hyperscaler

Hyperscaler is an AI-compute strategy game on Robinhood Chain. You stake **$VRAM** to bring a virtual GPU online, burn $VRAM on upgrades to raise its throughput, and earn **shards** of real Robinhood Stock Tokens: NVDA, MU, SNDK and QQQ.

The game runs continuously. Every hour is a **round**. The trading tax on $VRAM is swapped into the four Stock Tokens and fills the round's pot while it runs. When the round closes, the pot is split among the GPUs by the share of that hour's work each one did, you have fifteen minutes to claim, and whatever is not claimed rolls into the next pot. Shards redeem for the Stock Token itself, or for a USDG cash-out, at any time.

There is no proof of work and nothing runs on your computer. "Throughput" is a number on chain that your GPU earns with, every second, computed from your stake and upgrades.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Four things to know before you stake**

1. Your deposit comes back when you decommission your GPU, minus a 3% exit fee. Upgrades are paid in $VRAM and burned. They are gone whatever happens.
2. A round pays only what the fee stream brought in during that hour, plus whatever rolled over. A quiet hour is a small pot.
3. The operator can halt the game at any time and take back the unclaimed pots. Deposits always come back in full, and shards you have already claimed stay redeemable. This is stated on the site and in the terms.
4. Hyperscaler is not available to people in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, because Robinhood Stock Tokens are not.
{% endhint %}

## The pitch in one table

| | Hyperscaler |
| --- | --- |
| What you put in | A $VRAM stake per GPU (returned, minus the exit fee) and $VRAM you choose to burn on upgrades (not returned) |
| What you get out | Shards of NVDA, MU, SNDK and QQQ Stock Tokens, every hour |
| What decides your share | Your GPU's work in the round divided by everyone's work in that round |
| Where the pot comes from | The Pons trading tax on $VRAM, swapped into Stock Tokens on chain as it arrives, plus the previous round's unclaimed remainder |
| Randomness | None |
| Admin power over the live game | Pause (guardian), one-shot launch and halt (operator). No parameter changes, no upgrades to the contracts, no minting |
| How a round ends | On the clock, every hour. Claims are open for fifteen minutes; the rest rolls over |
